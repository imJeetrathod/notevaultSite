Bloop Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Bloop is a notes app for Windows. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • Your notes stay on your computer. Bloop has no account, no cloud service of its own, and no analytics or tracking.
  • The developer of Bloop never receives your notes or any information about you.
  • If you choose to connect Google Drive, Bloop sends a note to your Drive only when you ask it to.

What Bloop stores, and where

Everything is stored only on your PC:

  • Notes, their version history and pasted pictures — in a folder on your computer (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Bloop, or %USERPROFILE%\Bloop for the Microsoft Store version). Bloop also keeps up to 14 daily backups in the same folder.
  • Settings such as theme and font — in the app's local browser storage on your PC.

Bloop runs a small server that is reachable only from your own computer (127.0.0.1). It does not accept connections from other devices.

Things Bloop reads on your PC

  • Your desktop wallpaper — only to show it, blurred, as the app's background. It never leaves your computer.
  • Windows Notepad's unsaved tabs — only when you click "Import", to copy them into Bloop.

Google Drive (optional)

If you click Connect Google Drive:

  • You sign in with Google on Google's own page. Bloop never sees your Google password.
  • Bloop asks for two permissions: your email address (to show which account is connected) and access to only the files Bloop itself creates in your Drive (drive.file). Bloop cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on your PC. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • A note is uploaded to your Drive only when you click "Upload to Google Drive". Uploading it again updates the same Google Doc.
  • A note becomes viewable by others only when you click "Share", which turns on "anyone with the link can view" for that one document.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Data, and remove Bloop's access in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Bloop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the

Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the upload and sharing features you ask for, and is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone.

Children

Bloop is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Bloop leaves your notes on your PC so you don't lose them by accident. To delete them, delete the Bloop folder mentioned above. Documents you uploaded to Google Drive stay in your Drive until you delete them there.
